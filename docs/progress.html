What Eye4U actually does

done rev 1

Captured live, not from memory. The intro is a performance edited on beats: enormous flat vector shapes slamming through a full-bleed frame, three colours at a time, a 9px 'skip' against a screen-filling starburst. The nav is a diagram, not a menu — a photographic eye looks at an arc of beads. Three doors only. Takeaways: stage-not-document, giant flat shapes, cut-on-beat, committed palettes, identity-as-character, scale contrast, few doors.

Capture harness + engine substrate

done rev 1

tools/cap.mjs drives the real running site: screenshots, webm clips, and tiled contact sheets of N frames so motion can be judged as a storyboard. Engine is vanilla ES modules — scene router with hash deep-linking, WebAudio synth voices (no audio files), canvas layer helper, seeded PRNG, film grain, persisted visitor state. No build step, no framework, no external assets beyond Google Fonts.

Captured from the running site with Playwright — every image on this page is a real screenshot or a contact sheet of real frames, not a mockup.
